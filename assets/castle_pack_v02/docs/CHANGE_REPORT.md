## Deliberate, documented deviations
- Foliage objects (trees, bush, hedges) use a dark teal outline `#0C1A26` instead of navy `#090B1C` so greens do not look cut out. All other objects keep `#090B1C`.
- Silhouettes changed where the art changed (tree canopies, bush, hedges slightly, pointed gate arch interior shape, pointed door top, roof finial/trim, statue figure, fountain water now fills the basin, bench opening). Dimensions, pivots, footprints and collision footprints are unchanged.
- Pixel-art shading uses ordered (Bayer) dithering instead of random noise.

## Fixed v0.1 defects
- fountain_tiered_2x2: water had transparent diamond gaps (sampling bug).
- statue_guardian_generic: sword was detached from the figure.
- gate_arch_x: gold string course ran through the arch.
- Terrain: stone/grass/water patterns are now periodic on the 1-cell lattice so neighbouring tiles continue seamlessly (v0.1 patterns restarted per tile).

## Not regenerated (byte-identical to v0.1, hash-verified)
fountain_spray_anim, glow_warm, glow_cyan, light_shaft_01, magic_mote_anim, shadow_blob_64, scale_ruler_player_PLACEHOLDER, ui_panel_midnight_9slice. These were out of scope for this pass; the glows/spray were judged adequate in the preview, but they have not been improved.

## Known remaining limitations
- Procedural pixel art: lush and consistent, but not hand-painted anime quality. Foliage clump shapes are still somewhat round.
- No inner-corner grass/path transitions, pond shore rim, waterfall, bridge, or alternate roofs yet.
- Wall/tower tops are walkways without crenellation (adding merlons would change canvas dimensions).
- Doors are tuned for the LEFT face of wall_segment_x.
- Foliage has no sway/wind frames yet.
