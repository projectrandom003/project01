# Kael — The Weight of Those He Could Not Save

12 unique images; approximately 76.5 seconds. The narration is stored separately from the artwork in `kael_prologue_sequence.json`, so every line can be revised without editing an image.

## Emotional progression

1. **Peace** — Kael begins genuinely calm and asleep.
2. **Distant pain** — the kingdom burns and the battle unfolds; he remains off the battlefield.
3. **Empathy becomes physical** — the suffering begins to disturb his sleep.
4. **Helplessness** — the defenders fight on; the mother shields her child.
5. **Guilt** — Kael's hands clutch the sheets as if he could intervene. The fallen and the grieving child make him feel responsible, even though the dream has not explained why.
6. **Grief overwhelms him** — he trembles and cries while still asleep.
7. **Awakening** — only the final image shows him awake, gasping, with the feeling of failure still present.

## Visual treatment

The authored cinematic uses slow, restrained Ken Burns movement (subtle zoom and pan) on each still. The movement grows slightly more intimate as the nightmare progresses, especially on Kael's pained sleeping and tearful close-ups. There are no extra character animations fabricated from code and no Kael battlefield shots. The still images remain separate, with editable narration and timing.
